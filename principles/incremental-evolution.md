# Incremental Evolution

Prefer the simplest change that solves the current, evidenced problem. Let architecture, workflows, and the Toolkit evolve through validated needs rather than anticipated complexity.

Small, reversible steps make assumptions visible, reduce the cost of correction, and preserve the ability to learn from real use. A stable foundation does not mean a frozen implementation; it means that change happens within clear responsibilities and with deliberate evidence.

Avoid both extremes: do not preserve a weak design merely because it exists, and do not introduce abstraction, process, or structure for a future that has not yet earned it.
