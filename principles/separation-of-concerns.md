# Separation of Concerns

Clear boundaries reduce accidental coupling and make change safer. Product knowledge, reusable methods, agent responsibilities, focused skills, multi-stage workflows, task prompts, and output templates serve different purposes and should remain distinct.

Separate a concern according to the question it answers: product repositories explain a particular product; the Toolkit explains reusable development methods. Roles define responsibility, skills define how to perform a focused task, workflows define how work progresses, prompts compose context, and templates define output shape.

Boundary clarity does not require fragmentation. Keep related information together when it has one primary responsibility, and split material only when different responsibilities would otherwise compete or drift.
