# Knowledge as an Asset

Each project can produce more than a product: it can produce reusable engineering knowledge. Repeated decisions, validated workflows, templates, review practices, and problem-solving methods become assets when they can help the next project without carrying product-specific assumptions.

Capture reusable learning in the Toolkit when its value extends beyond the project that revealed it. Keep product facts in the product repository, and extract only the generalized method, rationale, or pattern.

This turns experience into compounding capability. The Toolkit should grow through evidence from real work, not through speculative accumulation.
