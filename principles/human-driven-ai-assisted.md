# Human-Driven, AI-Assisted

AI expands the developer's capacity for research, analysis, drafting, implementation, review, and documentation. It does not replace human ownership of purpose, judgment, trade-offs, or accountability.

The human defines the problem worth solving, decides which evidence is sufficient, evaluates alternatives, accepts risk, and makes the final decision. An agent should make reasoning visible enough to support that judgment rather than create the appearance of certainty or autonomy.

Use AI as a collaborative engineering capability: delegate bounded work, inspect its output, and preserve human control at consequential decision points.
